@f302256b6edd
In this case the prisoner Thomas Nielsen Silkjær and Christine Oline Enevoldsen are charged with breach of § 162 of the Penal Code. The prisoner was born on 10 January 1882, the accused on 17 May 1896, and neither of them is found to have been previously charged or punished. According to the prisoner’s and the accused’s concurring confessions, which agree with what has otherwise been established, the circumstances of the case are as follows: after the accused’s mother, Christiane, née Enevoldsen, had in April 1896 been widowed by her first husband, fisherman Christen Enevoldsen, of which marriage the accused was born about 1½ months after her father’s death, she entered on 5 May 1906 into a new marriage with the prisoner, in which marriage, which still subsists, 3 children have been born. At the same time as the prisoner has lived together with his wife in this marriage, until in December 1912 he secretly left home and went away together with an unmarried daughter of one of his neighbours, he has since the summer of 1911, at which time the accused was only some months over 15 years old, had intercourse several times with the accused [a remark on her physical development is not translated]; which relationship has resulted in the accused having become pregnant and, according to a midwife’s certificate, being expected to give birth in the month of May this year.

The prisoner never used violence or threats to get his will with the accused; the first time the prisoner asked her for intercourse, the accused made some weak objections to it, but gave in immediately afterwards when the prisoner reassured her that he would see to it that the relationship had no consequences, and that it did not matter when no one knew it; according to the accused’s statement it must be assumed that before the occasions immediately following she repeated her objections, but likewise quickly gave in, and gradually she gave up entirely every objection to the prisoner’s demands for intercourse.

The prisoner has admitted that he, who knew very well that the accused was only 15 years old when he first had intercourse with her, in view of her young age and his particular position as her stepfather <t1>must be said to have seduced her into the immoral relationship that took place between them</t1>; [a physical detail of his statement is not translated]. In accordance with what has been stated the prisoner and the accused are to be judged under § 162 of the Penal Code, in the accused’s case taken together with §§ 21 and 37, and the penalty is found in the circumstances to be fixed for the prisoner at hard labour in the house of correction for 18 months, and for the accused at simple imprisonment for one month. In view of the accused’s young age the execution of the sentence is further found, pursuant to § 20 of the provisional Penal Code of 1 April 1911, to be suspended, so that the penalty, provided the conditions laid down in the said provisions are fulfilled, lapses after the expiry of 5 years from the pronouncement of this judgment. The costs of the prosecution, including the fees to prosecutor and defence counsel given below, are to be laid on the prisoner and the accused in solidum. The appointed conduct of the case has been lawful.
