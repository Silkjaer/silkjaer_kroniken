Niels and Ejlert’s father, Thomas Nielsen Silkjær, trained at the Fishing Skippers’ School in Esbjerg in 1916—17. From 1923 he was master and owner, and with ANNE KIRSTINE he fished out of Esbjerg into the North Sea through the inter-war years.
