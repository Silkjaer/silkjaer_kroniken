Ten children grew up in and around Økjær. The story has followed three of them closely — Thomas in Esbjerg, Ane Johanne at Rækker Mølle, Jens Klaus on Djursland. That is why Silkjær is not one family in Husby but a net of families. Within a single generation the family was settled in Gudum, on Djursland, in the harbour streets of Esbjerg, in Fjand and in Vallensbæk west of Copenhagen. Some of the branches have lived far from one another for around a hundred years. They are equally related to the marsh.
