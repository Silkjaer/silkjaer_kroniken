Sentenced, 1913
@1cbaed96d8c3
On 14 April 1913 the Extraordinary Court of Ulfborg-Hind Hundred sentenced him to “Eighteen Months” of hard labour in the house of correction for breach of § 162 of the Penal Code — incest with his stepdaughter Kristine Oline Enevoldsen since the summer of 1911, when she was fifteen. At the examination on 22 March he himself admitted that he “must be said to have seduced her”. The prosecutor asked for the severest penalty the law allowed, the defence for the mildest; the sentence was read to him outside the gaol on 15 April — he declared himself “satisfied” and waived his right of appeal. He began serving his sentence in Ringkjøbing Gaol on 27 April; admitted to Vridsløselille on 3 July 1913 as prisoner no. 240. His counsel, the solicitor Kamp, applied for a pardon in July with the judge’s recommendation — the Ministry of Justice refused; at the King’s jubilee on 15 November 1913 he was pardoned by royal resolution of the same day, as one of seven prisoners recommended, and released at 4 p.m. [S120, S122, S168, S169, S170]

The case, 1911—13
@e6c852fb5752
The records preserve her words in reported form: before the first time, she said that it was wrong and that she might become pregnant — he brushed it aside. He admitted in court that he had seduced her. Even so, she herself was sentenced under § 162: one month’s simple imprisonment, reduced under the youth provisions §§ 21 and 37 and suspended under § 20 of the provisional Penal Code of 1 April 1911 — the court expressly gave her young age as the reason. The prosecutor had asked for the severest penalty the law allowed for her as well; the defence for a suspended sentence, and the court followed the defence. She was heavily pregnant and did not appear in court; the sentence was read to her and her guardian in person at the home at Årgab on 19 April by the parish summons officers — both declared themselves “satisfied with the Judgment”.
