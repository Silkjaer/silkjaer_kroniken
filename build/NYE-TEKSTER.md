# Nye tekster i den nye udgave

Alt herunder er tekst, som den nye udgave tilføjer omkring krøniken — knapper, overskrifter i scenerne,
billedtekster til de fotograferede sider og metabeskrivelser. Krønikens egen tekst er uændret og står ikke her.
Filen skrives af `build/ny.py` ved hver build; ret teksterne i `build/ui_ny.py` og `build/scenes_ny.py`.

## Kun på engelsk

**Reading this book** (forsiden)

> Danish surnames were not fixed until the twentieth century. A son of Thomas was Thomsen, a daughter of Jan was Jansdatter; the name changed with every generation. Many families also went by the name of the place they lived — a farm, a dune, a marsh — and that name could become a surname. Silkjær is such a name: first a place, then a byname, and from 1905 the family’s legal surname.

> A herred (hundred) was the old administrative and judicial district; a sogn (parish) was the church district within it, with its own parish register of baptisms, marriages and burials. Most of the sources in this book are those registers, the censuses and the court books of Ulfborg-Hind Hundreds.

> Silkjær is pronounced roughly “SILK-yair”. Kær means a low, wet marsh; the letters æ, ø and å are Danish vowels and are kept in all names.

**Oversættelsesnote** (kolofonen): This English edition is a translation of the Danish original. Quotations from the sources are given in English; where the original wording matters, the Danish is kept. Archive titles and place names stay in Danish.

**Link ved hver udeladelse i retsakterne:** »Read this passage in the Danish original« — 15 steder, hvert til samme blok på den danske side.

### Den nye udgaves scener

Scenerne bruger krønikens egne tekster som kort; det eneste nye er mærkaterne på tegningerne og kortene:

- Kapitel 5, tværsnittet: navnene »Anders Knudsen« og »Esper Jensen Ø.« ved de to mænd på årerne (fra teksten), mærkaterne ovenfor.
